What this place is

StonkFun launches memecoins anchored to real stocks. Each coin has a community and a stock it lives and dies by — and no game to play together. Stook Street is the game.

Every round here is a bet on where the coin's stock will be at a set time, quoted in the coin. $STOOK is anchored to the S&P 500, so $STOOK rounds ask where the S&P lands: at Friday's close, at noon tomorrow, in an hour. Not yes-or-no. You draw a line at the level you expect and buy it. Land on your line and you're paid the most; a band or two away, less; miss by a mile, nothing. The whole crowd's lines sit on one chart, so you can see what the street believes before you bet against it.

Rounds are opened by us, for coins we've brought onto the street. Nobody can paste a mint and spam a hundred markets. Coins get added one at a time, and the first is our own.

How the street works

  1. Pick a round. The S&P at Friday's close, at noon tomorrow, in an hour. Each round has 64 price bands laid out around where the index is when it opens.
  2. Draw a line. Click the band you expect. A line pays most on that band and one less for every band it misses by. Want a plain bet? Drag a range instead — it pays the same anywhere inside.
  3. Buy it, or sell out early. The price of a share is exactly what the crowd's odds say your payout profile is worth. Change your mind before the lock and sell at the current odds.
  4. Settlement is a number, not a vote. At the settlement second the market reads the stock's Pyth price — one specific update, chosen by a rule, so nobody picks it. It lands in a band. That band pays. The coin's own price is never part of it.
  5. Or be the house. Put your coin into a market's pool any time before it locks. You earn 80% of every fee from then on, you pay out when the crowd is right, and you can never lose more than you put in.

Every number on a market page is the program's own. The site quotes with the same maths the chain uses, to the last unit.

Being the house

Somebody takes the other side of every line. On Stook Street that's a pool anyone can join, in the market's coin, any time before the market locks.

YOU EARN

80% of every trade's fee, split by how much depth you put in — and only for trades after you joined. Half the protocol's cut goes to whoever settles the market, so it always gets settled.

YOU RISK

Your deposit, and never a unit more. It buys exactly as much depth as it can cover by itself. If the crowd draws its lines in the right place, the pool pays them; if it's wrong, the pool keeps their money.
